## Measurement findings

Report compliance was poor: 15 of 16 final responses were malformed under the
exact JSON contract, usually because the worker added prose or Markdown fences.
This correctly removed report and diagnosis credit and prevented full hidden
acceptance; it did not erase executable progress. Opus-high supplied the sole
valid report. The result therefore measures both implementation quality and
the ability to follow the public reporting contract. It must not be
retrospectively regraded by extracting JSON from malformed text.

The two B0 repetitions exposed material model-run variation on P06: 70 versus
100 executable points, a 30-point quality-v2 difference, and different
critical-error status. P04 and P08 were stable across the B0 pair; P05 was also
stable at its observed partial result. Four selected public families remain
development evidence and do not support a reserved-population claim.

For a future, separately authorised screen, first test schema-constrained final
output at the adapter boundary, such as Claude Code's JSON schema option, while
retaining exact raw bytes and the same failure classifications. Calibrate that
transport prospectively. Do not alter this screen or reinterpret its malformed
reports. Higher output limits are not the default remedy: the public report was
already capped at 16 KiB, and the xhigh failures consumed many tokens without
producing a valid bounded report.
