## Decision and limits

The candidate satisfied the automatic admission, actual frontier role,
end-to-end worker, ordinary route and clarification demonstrations. It did
not demonstrate a quality or acceptance benefit over S or B on any suitable
task pair, and cost and latency were substantially higher. The X3 control
reports show an unsupported completion claim in A's ordinary-worker path and
an untouched seeded claim on the clarify path. These results do not justify
promoting A or spending on X6 reserved comparison for this candidate. Retain
B0 as default and preserve the X6 cases unseen.

The four suitable cases saturated at full protected acceptance in every arm.
This comparison therefore cannot estimate whether Controller framing helps
on harder tasks where B or S fail. The v7 report score also incorporates
host evidence rather than independent worker-run probes. A new candidate
would need a new, prospectively frozen development design with realistic
baseline failures, explicit report provenance and a separate cost notice
before any further paid work. The present negative result must not be turned
into a positive claim by rescoring or relabelling the completed episodes.
