## Findings and disposition

1. **No policy-quality signal, blocking promotion.** All six paired quality
   deltas are zero and all ten episodes passed on the first call. The
   conditional coverage-repair call was never exercised live. Across its four
   matched tasks, coverage repair cost USD 0.373011 versus B0 USD 0.351739202
   (+6.0%) and took 192.68 versus 167.40 worker seconds (+15.1%). Across its
   two matched tasks, cross-component medium cost USD 0.297827001 versus B0
   USD 0.2280326 (+30.6%) and took 147.60 versus 101.55 worker seconds
   (+45.4%). These are descriptive single repetitions, not causal interval
   estimates. Disposition: retain B0 and stop paid expansion.
2. **Hidden grader permits false full credit, blocking promotion.** The D01
   grader imports editable actor code into its own Python process. The
   [zero-provider disposable probe](../../test/results/2026-09-26-worker-n7-grader-probe.json)
   changed only an allowed actor file and made a baseline score of 15 appear
   as 100 by replacing `json.dumps`; both baseline and forged actors passed
   the weak public check. `worker_q4u_grade.py` accepts that forged JSON
   without independently confirming case results. The sealed fixture and paid
   results were untouched. The paid records contain populated true case
   outcomes and sampled patches show ordinary repairs; no live exploitation
   was observed. Disposition: no qualification using this grader. A future
   quality campaign must isolate candidate execution from the trusted scorer
   and version its evaluator before spending.
3. **Canary dependency seal is incomplete, blocking promotion.** The
   [static import audit](../../test/results/2026-09-26-worker-n7-dependency-audit.json)
   found 18 directly imported local Python modules outside the manifest's
   16-file Python source seal, including `model_registry.py`,
   `worker_wsl_q3_adapter.py` and both structured-report helpers. The
   transitive static closure contains 25 unbound modules; dynamic imports,
   configuration and external runtime dependencies were outside this audit.
   `model_registry.py` also loads `src/model_registry.json` at runtime, and
   that file was not in the canary source seal. The manifest therefore cannot
   prove a complete unchanged execution dependency set. Disposition: do not
   retroactively edit the paid manifest; any future candidate needs a newly
   frozen complete dependency inventory. Controller X1 has the analogous
   manifest repair in its own plan.
4. **Evidence reach and host limits, non-blocking for B0 retention.** Four
   independent tasks with one repetition per arm cannot establish the planned
   uplift interval, and the four reserved mechanisms are too few to cure that
   by themselves. Requested low/medium CLI effort was recorded, while served
   effort was not attested. Graft's structural index was refreshed, but its
   deep semantic tier stopped with three unparseable summaries; no unchanged
   paid retry is warranted. Disposition: publish these limits and avoid an
   uplift claim.

The first three findings prevent candidate promotion. They do not identify a
new defect in the B0 shipping selector. The scorer and seal findings belong to
future evaluation infrastructure, not to a retroactive rewrite of the sealed
Q4U campaign. No additional paid call is justified by this review.
