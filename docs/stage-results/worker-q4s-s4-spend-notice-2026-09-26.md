# Q4S S4 six-family screen spend notice

Prepared 2026-09-26 UTC / Australia/Sydney, before any S4 model call. The
operator's standing authorisation covers paid calls in this project. This
notice applies only to the 18 frozen S4 episodes under the S2 parent manifest
and a source-bound S4 child manifest. The credential method is authenticated
Claude Code in WSL, not a Claude API key.

Each of six families has B0 A, B0 B and Sonnet-medium episodes from pristine
source. Every episode has a USD 4 local root allocation and at most three
calls: its frozen head, then the common Sonnet-low and Opus-high repair tail
only if needed. The combined S4 local admission allocation is **USD 72** and
the provider-call ceiling is **54**. A started call may exceed its allocation
before it stops; an overrun blocks later dispatch. There is no automatic
replay of an issued call or an uncertain episode.

The direct API-equivalent planning range is **USD 6-30** and elapsed time
**2-7 hours**, anchored to Q4's 16 calls at USD 9.849 and Q4R's three calls
at USD 1.666. S3's two small synthetic calls cost USD 0.1551532 as reported
by Claude Code; their lighter task does not justify narrowing the S4 range.
Per started attempt, plan for roughly 10,000-100,000 cache-created or cached
input tokens and 2,000-10,000 output tokens, with wider tails on complex
multi-module fixes and repairs. The distribution, cache mix and subscription
invoice impact are unknown. The runner will retain provider-reported usage
and cost for every settled call; unknown cost is never treated as zero.

Anthropic's official [Sonnet 5 pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/sonnet-5/overview)
on 2026-09-26 UTC was USD 2 per million ordinary input tokens and USD 10 per
million output tokens, with USD 2.50 per million five-minute cache-write
tokens and USD 0.20 per million cache-read tokens. The pinned
[Opus 5 pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5/overview)
was USD 5 input, USD 25 output, USD 6.25 five-minute cache write and USD
0.50 cache read per million tokens. These are API-equivalent rates, not a
Claude Code subscription invoice. One-hour cache writes, platform routing
or future price changes could alter that comparison.

Dispatch requires the S3 campaign to be sealed and complete, current WSL
host and Claude.ai subscription evidence, the frozen S2 and S4 manifests,
and exact approval for this S4 ceiling. No N5/Q5 reserved task, Controller
route or replacement of a selected family is in scope.
