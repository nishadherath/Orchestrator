# X5 candidate: review a public success only when risk remains observable

Date: 2026-09-30 Australia/Sydney. Status: **provider-free design**. The
first-failure-only development route has six one-call public successes and
zero eligible S/A pairs. This proposal is a new hypothesis, not a relaxation
of those immutable entry rules. B0 remains the shipping default and X6 is
sealed.

## Entry and treatment

Before any model call, freeze a fresh case list with public issue, source,
check, risk rubric, controls, protected oracle, analysis and budget. A case
may enter a post-success review only if a predeclared **public-only** rule
identifies a consequential unresolved premise and a specific verification
gap from the issue, source and public check. Record the source-cited risk
finding before the producer outcome is known. Hidden scores and historical
R01/F01–F03 outputs cannot select a case. Include ordinary clear-repair
controls and missing-decision controls to measure unnecessary reviews.

The producer gets one normal B0 Sonnet-low call. It must finish accepted on
the public check with a terminal, writer-stopped, identity-valid settled
receipt and no unresolved charge. The host independently reruns the public
check and hashes all public actor files. A first public failure follows its
own frozen route and is not converted to this review. For a qualifying
success, create single-use S and A roots from **identical accepted public
bytes**. Both receive the original issue, public check, risk finding and
producer edit summary, the same Sonnet-low worker call ceiling, tools and
acceptance contract. S asks one ordinary worker to review the residual
public risk. A first performs one bounded source-cited Controller
investigation and gives its concrete handoff to that same worker policy.
The producer cost is counted once; Controller assessment and roles belong
only to A. No hidden oracle is read until after both arms are settled.

## Measurement and stopping

Score host-verified functionality, critical edge cases, false success
claims, public evidence quality, cost and elapsed time separately. A public
pass in both arms is expected and cannot itself establish uplift. The new
development gate requires A to improve protected outcomes over S without
increasing critical errors or false-success claims, with a defensible
incremental cost and time. A small positive result is provisional. A larger
sample needs an X0-style power calculation, separate reserved cases, and
independent review before any production routing change.

Stop without opening X6 if fresh producers again reach 100/100 protected
quality, if the public-only risk rule selects ordinary controls too often,
if the Controller handoff lacks a source-cited discriminating check, if
receipts cannot be fully reconciled, or if A offers no measured gain over S.
No case is replayed to manufacture a hidden gap or a favorable pair.

## Mechanism boundary

The development helper must refuse cloning on any active writer, invalid
identity, open charge, source drift, hidden input, public failure or replay.
Accepted roots are terminal in `TaskExecutor`, so S and A require new roots
rather than attempting to resume the producer. Fake-provider and isolated
public-check tests precede a live campaign. The separate public risk rubric,
new case corpus and cost notice must be complete before the first paid call.

The development-only [accepted checkpoint helper](../../tools/controller_x5_public_risk_review.py)
now enforces one settled B0 acceptance, a unique public source citation bound
to a frozen risk digest, independent public success, exact file inventory,
single-use S/A copies and equal public bytes. A fake-provider test covers an
accepted producer and rejects an open charge, active writer, invalid identity,
missing risk decision, changed citation, public regression, extra actor file
and replay. This is mechanism evidence only; the public risk rule, fresh
effect corpus and live S/A review have not been qualified.
The final full offline harness passed **83/83** after the helper and its
negative controls were added. No provider call belongs to this design step.
