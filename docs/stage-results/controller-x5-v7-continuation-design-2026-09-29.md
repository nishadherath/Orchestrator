# X5 v7 development continuation design

Date: 2026-09-29 Australia/Sydney. Status: **provider-free design, no v7
provider call**. B0 remains the shipping default. The unseen X6 cases remain
sealed.

## Reason for a new comparison

The v6 pilot stopped after six episodes because its report rubric required a
schema and executed probes the Q4R worker host did not expose. All six
protected behaviour sets passed. The original receipts and scores remain
unchanged. A new grader separates functional acceptance, established by the
host's isolated public and protected checks, from report and investigation
quality. The real worker has Read, Edit, Write, Glob, Grep and Graft retrieval,
but no shell execution; a truthful unverified claim can coexist with a
host-verified repair.

## Predeclared outcomes

For X5-SHIP, X5-MIGRATE, X5-PAY and X5-FEAT, primary functional acceptance
requires every protected behavioural check, the public check, and no edit to
a protected actor file. The functional score is the weighted protected check
sum from 0 to 100. Critical failures and false completion claims are reported
separately. Secondary M/E/D/N/H quality retains weights 40/25/15/10/10:
M is protected behaviour, E is two host-verified causal discriminators,
D is a diagnosis of both mechanisms, N is a concrete next verification
step, and H is a claim consistent with the host-verified outcome. The report
parser accepts the actual B0 and S/A report shapes, including `unverified`
and `verification pending`; it records the raw claim and evidence count.
Host evidence is labelled as host evidence, not worker-run probes.

N01-D1 and C08-D2 retain their existing X3 protected rubric and isolated
grader. Their route and clarification outcomes are analysed separately from
the X5 functional acceptance metric.

## Historical and prospective halves

The six stopped v6 episodes cover X5-SHIP and X5-MIGRATE with matched B/S/A
arms. The new grader can read their frozen actor outputs and produce a
post hoc functional view without modifying receipts. The v7 continuation
will prospectively run only the unstarted schedule positions 7-18:
X5-PAY, X5-FEAT, N01-D1 and C08-D2, each in fresh matched B/S/A roots.
The actor packages, v6 public screen and worker policies stay the same.
The continuation will bind the v6 stop record and historical actor hashes
before launch, then require new single-use roots and sequential budget
reconciliation. Its analysis will label the first two task pairs post hoc
and the remaining four prospective. It will not describe the combination
as an independent replication or use it to promote a default.

The new local cap is USD 5 per episode, USD 60 across 12 episodes. The first
six observed episodes cost USD 4.129411806; their maximum is not reopened.
Historical costs suggest approximately USD 6-20 API-equivalent for the v7
continuation, with Controller path uncertainty. The USD 60 cap is a local
safety bound, not a forecast. This new run remains below the project's
USD 100 permission threshold and requires a dated cost notice before its
first provider call. Its stop rules include failed admission, unexpected
route, unresolved charge, host or source drift, or interrupted execution.

The X5 exit review must report four automatic Controller admissions across
the six task pairs, the actual frontier role from v6, at least one
host-verified Controller-to-worker result, clarification on C08-D2,
all-attempt accounting, matched quality/cost/latency and the report/tool
limitation. A favourable development result can only justify proposing X6.
