## Verification and limits

Before dispatch, the final offline harness passed **77/77** checks at HEAD
`15ecf66`; the manifest, historical S4/C1 seals, WSL runtime hashes,
Claude.ai subscription auth and 12-check Q4T capability probe passed. An
[egress audit](worker-q4t-s3-egress-audit-2026-09-26.md) confirmed that the
actor inventory contains only the frozen public upstream snapshots or the
authored synthetic task. Automatic approval review initially stopped the
launch before execution pending that audit; the evidence-backed retry was
accepted.

After completion, the journal and decision outer seals verified. Every one
of the 18 physical stopped workspaces passed the Q4T receipt, ledger, patch,
protected-source, executable-grade and quality validator. A read-only
recomputation matched the saved decision digest and `retain-b0` result.
The comparison must omit the saved file's outer `state_sha256` field because
the in-memory decision does not include that storage seal.

Zero invalid reports in these 22 calls supports the Q4T transport on this
screen, but it does not prove the earlier intermittent S03 structured-output
failure is eliminated: S03 was deliberately not replayed, and the sample is
small. The family scores are paired within this frozen corpus, not an estimate
of deployment-wide performance. Served reasoning effort remains unobserved.
