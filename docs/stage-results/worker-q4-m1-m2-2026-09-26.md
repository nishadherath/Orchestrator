# Q4 M1/M2 measurement and dispatch result

Date: 2026-09-26 Australia/Sydney. Status: **M1 and M2 complete; M3 later
completed with no candidate**. This stage made no Claude provider call and did not change the
shipping B0 policy. It implements the prospective measurement contract and
the controlled development boundary required before the approved public M3
screen.

## Delivered behaviour

M1 adds a deterministic 100-point quality contract: 60 points for required
behaviour, 20 for preserved invariants, 10 for diagnosis and 10 for reporting.
Every predicate binds to a public obligation. Exact wording or exception types
are eligible only when the public contract requires them. The evaluator keeps
behaviour acceptance, full hidden acceptance, root acceptance gaps,
unsupported completion, critical errors and report observability separate.
A missing or malformed report loses report credit without erasing executable
progress.

The evaluation adapter retains only the final structured result needed for
grading and binds it to the invocation, task, prompt, stream, requested cell
and collected revision. The ordinary worker adapter still retains no model
text. Wrong revision bindings, modified report bytes, missing terminal events
and contradicted success claims fail closed.

M2 adds experimental policy `Q4-first-cell-v2`. Every candidate arm uses the
same repair tail, `worker-sonnet-low` followed by `worker-opus-high`; only its
first cell varies. The historical `N5-experimental-v1` policy remains
supported for its frozen evidence. Admission rejects a reordered, duplicated
or substituted repair tail before dispatch.

The versioned Q4 WSL boundary admits exactly `worker-sonnet-low`,
`worker-sonnet-xhigh` and `worker-opus-high`. Its installed launcher preserves
the restricted environment, actor isolation, writer-stop and protected-source
checks. The provider-free probe exercised all three identities, two collected
edits, structured report binding and unsupported-cell rejection.

P06 and P08 material. Each family passed reference and materially different

failure were detected independently. Public-answer copying failed. The P05
timer-sensitive baseline and reference were each graded ten times with stable

provider-free boundary. They do not show that Sonnet-xhigh or Opus-high
improves live task quality, and they do not estimate reserved-population
performance. B0 remains the shipping policy.
